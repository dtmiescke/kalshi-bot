-- Quick guide --
P&L = dollars won/lost after fees. ROI = profit/loss as a share of money committed.
CLV (closing-line value) = whether your price was better than the final market price; positive is generally a good sign, not proof of profit.
Prediction error / Brier = how far predicted chances were from results; 0 is perfect. Compare similar markets and use plenty of settled predictions.
Signal weights = how strongly the bot currently uses each input; they are not a grade by themselves.
Calibration = when the bot says 60%, does it happen about 60% of the time? Small samples can mislead.
